# Knight Club

## Register

product

## Users

Chess players who want an approachable game against a bot and want to understand the quality of each move.

## Product Purpose

Provide a polished online chess board, adjustable computer opponents, and unlimited browser-based Stockfish analysis. Success means completing a game and understanding how to improve.

## Brand Personality

Focused, welcoming, quietly competitive. The user chose a familiar dark chess app with green accents, referencing Chess.com.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-first pages, unnecessary onboarding, subscription gates, and visual clutter that competes with the board.

## Design Principles

- Playing is the primary action; keep the board central.
- Every move is an opportunity for useful feedback.
- Show real engine results and honest loading states.
- Let beginners discover help without slowing experienced players.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-operable squares and controls, visible focus states, readable contrast, labels alongside move-quality colors, reduced-motion support, and responsive phone layouts.
